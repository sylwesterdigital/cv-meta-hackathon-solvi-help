Robot commands and syntax

Address one robot using @Paris, a named group such as @Elox @Jessica, or broadcast with @All. Mentions respond in order, up to six robots per message; robots may choose not to speak if they have nothing relevant to add.

@Paris help
@Paris list voices
@Paris use a female voice
@Paris you are female
@Paris find the grey sofa and sit
@Paris go to Jessica
@Paris walk 4 meters and return 3 times
@Paris turn right 90 degrees then walk 2 meters
@Paris explore 4 times
@Paris stop
@Elox how is your mission going?
@Elox @Jessica compare your plans
@Elox @Jessica discuss whether vitamin C helps hair loss; compare evidence, pros and cons, then agree or disagree
@All offer one helpful thought each
